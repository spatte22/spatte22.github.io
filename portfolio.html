NiCE CXone: Getting Started
Guide for Developers Integrating with NiCE CXone
Audience: Devs + Product Managers
Technical Level: High
Output: HTML

CXone is industry-leading contact center software (CCaaS). They have a thriving community of integrators, including both customers and partners. Integrators had minimal self-service resources and constantly asked Support about registering their app with NiCE and setting up the correct form of API authentication.

To solve this, I worked with a pair of lead engineers to create this getting started guide. Our approach included the following:

  • The guide needed to be instructional and task-oriented. This would help lead devs through the steps instead of obligating them to figure it out with minimal documentation.
  • The guide would work in tandem with an Authentication and Authorization Overview, which we created to be a more in-depth discussion of how API authentication worked with CXone.
  • The guide would prioritize a planning stage, which would also answer the FAQs that support engineers would handle.

The lead engineers informed me that this guide reduced their API-related support requests by 90%.

SSO Implementation Guide
Technical Instructions
Audience: Security Admins
Technical Level: Mid
Output: PDF

This introduces the concept of single sign-on (SSO) and provides instructions for setting it up for financial institutions. The idea is that account managers could share this PDF with customers to a) inform themselves on the general SSO capabilities, and b) lead techncial personnel through the setup steps.

The following are unique aspects of this guide:

  • A "tl;dr summary" at the beginning allowed anyone familiar with SSO to get the key details right away. This allowed them to skip directly to the setup sections.
  • Examples and diagrams are key for this guide. They help illustrate the different parties involved in an SSO, which can sometimes be confusing. They also help paint the picture of how SSO are valuable.
  • No single SSO setup was exactly the same. Factors like the customer's chosen identity provider impacted the exact implementation steps. Therefore, we relied on a substantial preparation section to help customers plan their setup and obtain or generate the necessary information. After doing so, customers could easily follow the generic setup steps.
