Agents Quickstart Guide

This page gives you a quick introduction to <PRODUCT>. It covers the basics of the system that you will use on a day-to-day basis.

The purpose of this page is to get you working in <PRODUCT> as soon as possible.

What is <PRODUCT>?

<PRODUCT> is a communication system that you open in your web browser. Its main purpose is to help you interact with customers (or “contacts”). The core features let you send text messages, live chats, or handle phone calls and video calls. It also has many other features that make the communication more effective.

For example:

  • When talking to a customer (or “contact”), you can switch between channels. Imagine you're in a chat. You could also send a text message to the contact.
  • You can schedule appointments.
  • Both you and customers (or “contacts”) can share your screen. 
  • You can verify a contact’s identity. This ensures that they are a real human (not a bot) and they are who they say they are.
  • You can sign documents online with a contact, like an application to open a new account.
  • and more…

Set Up Your <PRODUCT> User Account

The following instructions help you set up the basics of your <PRODUCT> user account.

  1. Administrators of your <PRODUCT> system are called “domain managers”. A domain manager can create an <PRODUCT> account for you. After doing so, you receive an email with an invitation to activate the account. Click the link in the email to activate the account and set a password.

  2. Work through this page to customize your user account settings.

  3. (Optional) You can select a default department. This may not be necessary, depending on your system setup. Ask your domain manager.

Handle a Contact

The following instructions give the basic steps for handling a contact. It does not cover the many possible features or tasks that you may need to perform.

  1. First, ensure that your user status is available.

  2. When the system offers you an interaction request, a pop-up displays:

  3. Accept the interaction request. After accepting, your agent workspace updates to the contact handling view with the messenger and conversation history.

  4. Interact with the contact and assist them to the best of your abilities. This may require you to use certain add-on features to sign documents. Not all features may be set up or enabled for you, however, you can do things like:

    • Use a variety of features in the messenger. You can verify a contact’s phone number, send documents to sign, change to a different channel, and so forth.

    • Read messages from the contact or system messages, access uploaded documents, or click links in the conversation history. If the interaction was started from a webpage (like from your company's website), you can click the link to see which page they came from.

    • View information about the contact or verify their identity from the side panels.

    • Share your screen, view the contact’s screen, and other features in the tabs.

  5. When you’re done helping the contact, click the Complete button to complete the interaction.

  6. (Optional) To finish an interaction, your domain manager may require you to complete “after-call work”, like writing notes about the interaction or applying tags. You can manually open the Interaction tags & notes side-panel, or the after-call work (ACW) panel will open automatically.

Start an Outbound Interaction

Usually, contacts are initiating conversations with you. However, you may occasionally want to start the conversation. Depending on the features your system has, you can start an outbound text, call a video room at one of your company’s physical locations, or make an outbound call.

To do so:

  1. In your agent workspace, click the add button .
  2. Select the type of outbound interaction you want to start:
    • New conversation = text message
    • Video room outbound = video call a physical location
    • Phone dialer = phone call

  3. Depending on what you chose, the next step is to enter the contact details for the person you want to interact with. For example, if you’re making a phone call, enter the phone number you want to call.

  4. You may need to choose a department. Interactions are handled and categorized under a specific department. For example, if you’re a loans officer and want to start a text thread with a loan applicant, you would choose the loans department to which you’re assigned.

  5. Start the interaction.
